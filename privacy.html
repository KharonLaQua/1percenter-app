Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1 Percenter is a private tool used only by its owner. This policy covers the Google account data it accesses.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads and updates the owner's own Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Drive, Docs and Sheets through Google's APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as updating the owner's spreadsheets or reading the owner's documents. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train general AI models.

1 Percenter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and removal

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
